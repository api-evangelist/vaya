# Vaya

> Vaya is an AI-powered Vedic astrology platform from Anuvaya Labs Technologies Pvt Ltd (New Delhi, India) that connects users with classically trained Parashari astrologers for live chat consultations, alongside free tools for Kundli (birth chart), horoscope, dasha, kundli matching, marriage prediction, navamsa, darakaraka, and panchang. Founded by Maahin Puri and Nitesh Kumar Niranjan (previously co-founders of travel-tech startup Atlys), Vaya is backed by Accel, Arkam Ventures, and Weekend Fund. The product ships as iOS and Android apps; the company publishes no public API or developer program.
